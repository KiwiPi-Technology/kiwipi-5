## Firmware installation

Use the firmware upgrade manual that matches your board and image. The developer guide references RKDevTool and DriverAssistant for Windows. Obtain the required tools from the [official resources](downloads.md#manuals-tools-and-source-archives).

## Hardware development

Match schematics and connector assignments to the revision printed on your board. For help resolving a revision or configuration mismatch, [contact KiwiPi](https://kiwipi.com/contact-us/).

## Software development

The developer guide covers Android 15 and kernel compilation. Download the corresponding SDK from the [official source folder](https://drive.google.com/drive/folders/1t1zYssr70_ai85JC8QEf8XBuI-8L0Mc7) and follow its build instructions and component licenses.
